Summary

  • Your projects, datasets and analysis results are stored in your browser, not on our servers.
  • We use no third-party analytics, advertising pixels, fonts or CDNs.
  • We do not sell personal information and do not use your content to train AI models.

Local-first data

The Cerebro OS workspace stores data in your browser's IndexedDB and local storage. Analyses are computed on your device. Participant-level rows you load into the Analysis Lab are never transmitted by the application. Data leaves your device only when you explicitly export it (for example, as CSV, BibTeX, RIS, Markdown or JSON).

Because data is stored locally, clearing your browser's site data will delete it. You are responsible for exporting and backing up your work.

Network requests made by the workspace

When you run a literature or dataset search, your browser sends your search query directly to the relevant public research API. A Content-Security-Policy restricts the workspace to an allowlist of these services:

  • Europe PMC
  • NCBI E-utilities (PubMed)
  • ClinicalTrials.gov
  • OpenNeuro
  • DANDI Archive

Those services process your queries under their own privacy policies. Your local datasets are not included in these requests.

Server logs

The servers that deliver the Cerebro OS website and application files may record standard request metadata (such as IP address, user-agent, requested path and timestamp) for security, abuse prevention and operations. [Retention period: e.g. 30 days.] These logs are not used for advertising or profiling.

If you self-host Cerebro OS, your organisation operates the server and controls these logs.

Cookies & tracking

We do not use tracking cookies. The site stores your preferred color theme in local storage under the key cerebro-theme; it never leaves your browser.

AI training

We do not use your content to train, fine-tune or evaluate AI models, and we take technical measures to prevent OpenAI's crawlers and agents from accessing this site. See our Security page and Terms.
