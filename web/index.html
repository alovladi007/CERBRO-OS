Profile a cohort, compare groups and model outcomes without exporting to another tool. Effect sizes and confidence intervals are reported alongside every p-value, and multiple-comparison corrections are one click away.

Built for rigor

Numbers you can defend in peer review.

Research software should show its work. Cerebro OS is designed so that every figure in your manuscript can be traced back to a method, a version and an exact input.

01 · TRANSPARENCY

Named methods

Results state the exact estimator, test, correction and CI method used — never a black-box “score”.

02 · VERIFICATION

Tested against SciPy

Every engine is checked against SciPy/NumPy reference values in an automated test suite that runs on each change.

03 · PROVENANCE

Input fingerprints

Each run records method version, timestamp, inputs and a SHA-256 fingerprint, exportable as JSON.

04 · ASSUMPTIONS

Explicit assumptions

Equal-variance, normality and independence assumptions are surfaced with the result, not buried in docs.

Security & privacy

Your research is not training data.

Cerebro OS is local-first by design, and it takes a deliberate stance on AI crawlers: OpenAI's agents are refused at every layer we control.

  • Data stays on your deviceProjects live in your browser's IndexedDB. Participant-level rows never leave the device unless you export them yourself.
  • Strict egress allowlistA Content-Security-Policy permits network calls only to the named research APIs — Europe PMC, NCBI, ClinicalTrials.gov, OpenNeuro, DANDI.
  • OpenAI agents refused at the serverGPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot and other OpenAI agents are blocked by user-agent and by OpenAI's published IP ranges, disallowed in robots.txt, and served noai / X-Robots-Tag directives.
  • The app can never call OpenAIOpenAI endpoints are blocked by CSP and by a runtime fetch guard — nothing in the workspace can send your work there.
  • No framing, no trackersOther sites cannot embed Cerebro OS. No analytics, no third-party fonts, no ad pixels. Our Terms prohibit using content for AI model training.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Projects, datasets and analysis results are stored in your browser's IndexedDB and computed locally. Participant-level rows never leave your device unless you export them. The only network requests the workspace makes are searches you run against the named research APIs (Europe PMC, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, OpenNeuro, DANDI) — and those contain your search terms, not your data.

Is Cerebro OS a medical device?

No. Cerebro OS is for research use only. It is not a medical device, it does not provide clinical decision support, and it must not be used to diagnose, treat or manage individual patients.

Why do you block OpenAI?

Unpublished research, protocols and analyses are valuable and often confidential. We do not want this site or anything produced in the workspace to be crawled, summarised or used for model training by OpenAI's agents, so we refuse GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot and related agents by user-agent and published IP range, disallow them in robots.txt, and prevent the app from ever contacting OpenAI endpoints.

Can I self-host?

Yes. Cerebro OS deploys as plain static files, via a zero-dependency Node server, or as a Docker container. It is stateless on the server side, horizontally scalable and CDN-friendly. Lab / Enterprise plans include self-hosting assistance.

What methods are validated, and how?

Every analysis engine — statistics, regression, survival, signal processing, meta-analysis and power — is checked against reference values computed with SciPy and NumPy in an automated test suite. Results name the exact method used so you can reproduce them in other software.

Validation against reference software is not a regulatory certification; always review results in the context of your study design.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Cerebro OS is an installable progressive web app. Once loaded, the Analysis Lab, protocols and your saved projects work without a connection. Literature and dataset searches naturally need network access.

How do I export or back up my work?

Export references as BibTeX, RIS or CSV, protocols as Markdown, and analysis provenance as JSON. Because data lives in your browser, we recommend exporting regularly — clearing site data in your browser will remove local projects.
