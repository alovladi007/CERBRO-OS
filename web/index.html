02 — Platform

Every stage of a study, in one workspace.

Search portals, screening spreadsheets, repository websites and statistics packages, replaced by a single environment designed around neuroscience and neurology research.

Federated evidence search

One query across Europe PMC, PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov, de-duplicated by DOI and PMID.

  • Filters and abstract preview
  • Saved searches with “new since last run”
  • Export to BibTeX, RIS and CSV

Systematic-review screening

Keyboard-driven title and abstract screening with include, exclude and maybe decisions — each with a recorded reason.

  • Decision reasons kept with each record
  • PRISMA 2020 flow diagram generated automatically

Dataset discovery

Live search of OpenNeuro and DANDI Archive, plus a curated directory of major neuroscience repositories.

  • EBRAINS, NEMAR, NeMO, brainlife and more

Analysis Lab

Statistics, regression, survival, signal processing, meta-analysis and power — computed in your browser, with heavy work moved to a Web Worker.

  • Cohort profiling, descriptives, histograms, box plots
  • Every engine checked against SciPy and NumPy

Study protocols

Templates for randomised trials (SPIRIT-style), observational studies (STROBE-style) and systematic reviews (PRISMA-P-style).

  • Completeness tracking
  • Markdown export

  • Exportable JSON provenance
  • Installable, and analysis works offline

Results that show their work.

Named methods
Results state the exact estimator, test, correction and CI method used — never an opaque score.
Explicit assumptions

Each run records method version, timestamp, options, inputs and a SHA-256 fingerprint. Identical inputs give an identical fingerprint.

04 — Security & privacy

Your research is not training data.

Cerebro OS is local-first by design, and it takes a deliberate position on AI crawlers: OpenAI’s agents are refused at every layer we control.

  • Data stays on your device

    Projects live in your browser’s IndexedDB. Participant-level rows never leave the device unless you export them yourself.

  • Strict egress allowlist

    A Content-Security-Policy permits network calls only to the named research APIs — Europe PMC, NCBI, ClinicalTrials.gov, OpenNeuro and DANDI.

  • OpenAI agents refused at the server

    GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot and other OpenAI agents are blocked by user-agent and by OpenAI’s published IP ranges, disallowed in robots.txt, and served noai and X-Robots-Tag directives.

  • The workspace cannot call OpenAI

    OpenAI endpoints are outside the CSP allowlist and rejected by a runtime fetch guard, so nothing in the workspace can send your work there.

  • No framing, no trackers

    Other sites cannot embed Cerebro OS. No analytics, third-party fonts or advertising pixels. Our Terms prohibit using site or platform content for AI model training.

Cerebro OS does not currently hold HIPAA, SOC 2 or ISO certifications. It is designed so that sensitive data need not leave your machine.

07 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Projects, datasets and analysis results are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB and computed locally. Participant-level rows never leave your device unless you export them. The only network requests the workspace makes are searches you run against the named research APIs (Europe PMC, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, OpenNeuro, DANDI) — and those contain your search terms, not your data.

Is Cerebro OS a medical device?

No. Cerebro OS is for research use only. It is not a medical device, it does not provide clinical decision support, and it must not be used to diagnose, treat or manage individual patients.

What methods are validated, and how?

Every analysis engine — statistics, regression, survival, signal processing, meta-analysis and power — is checked against reference values computed with SciPy and NumPy in an automated test suite (see Table 1). Results name the exact method used so you can reproduce them in other software.

Validation against reference software is not a regulatory certification; always review results in the context of your study design.

Why do you block OpenAI?

Unpublished research, protocols and analyses are valuable and often confidential. We do not want this site, or anything produced in the workspace, to be crawled, summarised or used for model training by OpenAI’s agents. We refuse GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot and related agents by user-agent and published IP range, disallow them in robots.txt, and prevent the app from contacting OpenAI endpoints.

Can I self-host?

Yes. Cerebro OS deploys as plain static files, through a zero-dependency Node server, or as a Docker container. The server side is stateless, horizontally scalable and CDN-friendly. Lab / Enterprise plans include self-hosting assistance.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Cerebro OS is an installable progressive web app. Once loaded, the Analysis Lab, protocols and your saved projects work without a connection. Literature and dataset searches need network access.

How do I export or back up my work?

Export references as BibTeX, RIS or CSV, protocols as Markdown, and analysis provenance as JSON. Because data lives in your browser, export regularly — clearing site data in your browser removes local projects.
